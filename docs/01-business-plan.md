# DataShield India: Business Plan

> Find where your personal data is exposed, and remove it, only with your permission.

## Origin of the idea
Idea from a creator video: people's phone numbers and details appear on random websites; a platform finds exposed data, lets the user choose which listings to act on, and handles removal with the user's permission.

## Problem
Phone numbers, emails and addresses are scraped onto listing and lookup sites, driving spam, scams and harassment. Individuals do not know where they are exposed or how to demand removal.

## Solution
With the user's consent, scan configured sources for their data. Show each listing. The user selects which ones to act on; for those, DataShield sends a removal request citing their right to erasure under the DPDP Act, 2023, tracks responses, and follows up or escalates. No listing is touched without per-listing, revocable consent.

## Target users
- Individuals receiving spam/scam calls
- Women and public-facing professionals worried about doxxing
- Families (add parents/children)
- Later: businesses protecting employee/executive data

## Revenue model
- Annual subscription with continuous monitoring (core)
- One-time cleanup package
- Family plan
- B2B/employee-protection plans

## Key risks
- Scanning itself must be lawful and not violate source sites' terms or scrape what we should not
- We hold sensitive identifiers: breach would be catastrophic
- Sites ignore requests; whack-a-mole re-listing
- Impersonation: someone requesting removal of another person's data
- Law and enforcement mechanisms are still evolving

## Success metrics
- Findings per user
- Consent-to-removal conversion
- Removal success rate and time-to-removal
- Re-listing rate
- Subscription retention
