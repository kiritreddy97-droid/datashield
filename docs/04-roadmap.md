# DataShield India: Roadmap

## Phase 0 - Legal and validate (weeks 1-4)
- Legal review of scanning approach and DPDP obligations
- Landing page; user interviews
- List candidate sources and their removal channels

## Phase 1 - MVP (weeks 5-10)
- Consent, scan, findings and removal flows (this repo's backend)
- OTP verification of identifiers
- 3-5 source scanners

## Phase 2 - Monitor (months 3-6)
- Continuous rescans and alerts
- Mobile app, family plan
- Escalation playbooks

## Phase 3 - Expand (months 7-12)
- B2B employee protection
- More sources, partnerships

Timings are planning estimates for a small team; re-plan at the end of each phase.
