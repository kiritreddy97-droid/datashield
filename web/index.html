DataShield India

Find where your personal data is exposed, and remove it, only with your permission.

The problem

Phone numbers, emails and addresses are scraped onto listing and lookup sites, driving spam, scams and harassment. Individuals do not know where they are exposed or how to demand removal.

How it works

With the user's consent, scan configured sources for their data. Show each listing. The user selects which ones to act on; for those, DataShield sends a removal request citing their right to erasure under the DPDP Act, 2023, tracks responses, and follows up or escalates. No listing is touched without per-listing, revocable consent.

Who it is for

  • Individuals receiving spam/scam calls
  • Women and public-facing professionals worried about doxxing
  • Families (add parents/children)
  • Later: businesses protecting employee/executive data
